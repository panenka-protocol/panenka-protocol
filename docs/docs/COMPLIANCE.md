# Compliance gate (project rule)

Effective Sep 24, 2026. This document is binding project policy for the hackathon build.

## Context

India's Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act, 2025 defines "online money game" to cover games of skill AND chance where users pay fees, make deposits, or stake tokens in expectation of monetary enrichment, and prohibits offering, advertising and related money transfers. Exact commencement/enforcement detail and how devnet deployments are treated are UNVERIFIED as of this writing.

## Rules for this build

1. Contest money stays DEVNET-ONLY. No real-SOL escrow, no winner payouts, and no protocol-fee collection on any network carrying real value.
2. No mainnet contest launch. Ever, without a counsel-reviewed redesign.
3. Never claim - in docs, pitch, videos, or copy - that the "skill-based staked contest" framing makes real-money PvP contests compliant in any jurisdiction. The framing describes mechanics; it is not a legal position.
4. Mainnet attestation memos (no value transfer) are fine.
5. The agent swarm, devnet contest demo, videos and submission proceed unchanged.

## Framing discipline (all public copy)

Use: escrow, programmatic settlement, oracle-verified, protocol fee, skill-based staked contest.
Never use: bet, wager, odds, gambling.

## Open decision

Devnet-only demo as-is vs. counsel-reviewed redesign for any real-money future. Owner: Raghu. To be decided with legal counsel before anything post-hackathon touches mainnet value.
